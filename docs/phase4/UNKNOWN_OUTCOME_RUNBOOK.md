# Unknown provider outcome runbook

An operation that may have crossed the stdio boundary is retained as `unknown`. Do not retry a
turn, fork, thread creation, or compaction solely because its response was lost. Keep the Phase 2
scheduler held, advance the owner epoch on recovery, reconcile only by an authorized native
identity, and quarantine when continuity or terminal evidence is ambiguous. A late response cannot
dispatch an action after retirement. Local cleanup/retirement is separate from any provider-side
erasure and is never reported as forgetting.
